Chronicle

The ETF Journey (1990 – Present)

From a single benchmark Canadian fund to a multi-trillion dollar decentralized revolution.

March 1990

Genesis: TIPS 35

The Toronto 35 Index Participation Units debuted on the Toronto Stock Exchange—the first true exchange-traded index basket.

January 1993

SPDR S&P 500 (SPY)

State Street launched SPY in the US. Today, it remains among the most heavily traded and liquid exchange-traded funds in the world.

Dec 2001 / Jan 2002

India Enters: Nifty BeES

Allotted in Dec 2001 and listed on NSE on Jan 8, 2002 by Benchmark Mutual Fund, introducing Indian retail to exchange-traded indexing.

2015 & Beyond

Institutional Scale & Debt

EPFO initiated ETF allocations in August 2015, paving the way for Bharat Bond target-maturity debt, factor indices, and precious metals.

Educational Model

The Core-and-Satellite Framework (Illustrative Model)

An academic illustration of diversified multi-asset allocation. Not personalized investment advice.

Illustrative 70-80% Core Exposure
Surrounded by an illustrative 20-30% Satellite strategic layer.

The 70-80% Core: Foundation Index Beta

Standard models utilize broad market index vehicles (Nifty 50, BSE Sensex, S&P 500) paired with Target Maturity Debt ETFs. Captures long-term economic growth with low expense overhead.

The 20-30% Satellite: Tactical Allocation

Investors often supplement core holdings with Factor-based ETFs (Momentum, Low Volatility) or precious metals (Gold/Silver) to address personal risk tolerance and inflationary hedges.

Execution Tip: Always place Limit Orders rather than Market Orders during trading hours, verifying current iNAV to minimize bid-ask spread slippage.
