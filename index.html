Chronicle

The ETF Journey (1990 – Present)

From a single benchmark Canadian fund to a multi-trillion dollar decentralized revolution.

1990

Genesis: TIPS 35

The Toronto 35 Index Participation Units debuted on the Toronto Stock Exchange—the first true exchange-traded index basket.

1993

SPDR S&P 500 (SPY)

State Street launched SPY in the US. Today, it remains the most traded and liquid financial instrument on planet Earth.

2001

India Enters: Nifty BeES

Benchmark Mutual Fund pioneered India's first ETF, tracking the Nifty 50, introducing Indian retail to exchange-traded indexing.

2020 & Beyond

Active, Factor & Bharat Bond

Massive expansion into target maturity debt (Bharat Bond), Smart Beta (Momentum/Low-Vol), physical gold/silver, and EPFO mandates.

Strategic Allocation

The Core-and-Satellite Growth Engine

How modern investors structure low-cost portfolios for generational wealth creation.

Foundation 70-80% Core Assets
Surrounded by a 20-30% Satellite tactical layer to target excess returns (Alpha).

The 70-80% Core: Low-Cost Resilience

Allocate broad market index ETFs (Nifty 50, Nifty Next 50, S&P 500) combined with Sovereign G-Sec or Target Maturity Debt ETFs. Zero fund manager bias, ultra-low fees, and participation in national economic GDP growth.

The 20-30% Satellite: Tactical Alpha

Use Smart Beta / Factor ETFs (Nifty 200 Momentum 30, Low Volatility) or thematic rotations (IT, Infra, Banking) plus 5-10% Physical Gold/Silver ETFs for hedge protection during inflationary spikes.

Execution Tip: Always place Limit Orders rather than Market Orders during live hours, verifying current iNAV to avoid brief liquidity bid-ask slippage.
